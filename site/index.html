Kultr Download

Your library, mixed like a DJ.

Kultr is a music player for your Navidrome server, in the browser, on Android and on iPhone. On a phone or a computer it plays the music stored there too.

Download Open in your browser

What it does

InjeKt

Tracks blend like a DJ set: tempo-matched, landing on the downbeat, with the basslines swapped. Or plain crossfades and gapless albums.

Karousel

Tap shuffle twice, and when the queue runs out, music like what was playing keeps coming.

Stations

Endless mixes from your own library, such as Night Drive or Deep Focus, or built from your own rules.

Energy planner

Draw how the next hour should feel, calm to peak, and get a set whose tracks follow the line and mix.

Playlists that build themselves

At the bottom of a playlist, songs that would fit. Add one and the suggestions turn to songs like it.

Your own files

Play the music stored on your phone or computer, with no server and no account.

Right in the notification

Switch between shuffle, repeat, repeat one and Karousel, and like the song, right beside the controls.

Android

Instant and offline

Your whole library is kept on the device, so browsing and search are instant. Download anything to play offline.

The same everywhere

Plays, favourites and ratings live on your Navidrome, and settings move between the browser, Android and iPhone.

Get Kultr

Browser

Any current browser

The full app, nothing to install. It connects straight to your Navidrome, and you can install it as an app from the browser.

Open web.kultr.cc

Or host it yourself.

Android

Android 8.0 or later

Install the APK once; after that, Kultr updates itself from inside the app.

Download the APK

iPhone

iOS 26 or later

Not on the App Store: you install it yourself with a free Apple ID. Add Kultr’s source to SideStore or AltStore and updates come to you.

Download the IPA
